1 · Team key

The team key turns each URN into the same Patient ID for every clinician, every month. Use the key your team agreed. It is never saved or sent; without it, IDs cannot be traced back to a patient.

2 · Activity report

Export the Allied Health activity report from SAHR (.xls or .xlsx) for your date range. You can add several clinicians' reports at once.
